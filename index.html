About

Data systems that stay accurate when the data falls short.

I am a PhD candidate in computer science at the University of Minnesota, advised by Mohamed Mokbel, working in data management and data systems.

My work covers the whole pipeline: cleaning raw data at scale, inferring what is missing, answering queries from coarse aggregates, calibrating existing methods as black boxes, and benchmarking them on equal footing.

Research

Dissertation

Towards Accurate Open-Source ETA

Accurate travel times are locked behind private trip data. My dissertation delivers accurate ETA from whatever trip data you have, in three parts.

  1. ETAlon measures every method at every data level.
  2. CoarseETA fills the gap at the level cities publish.
  3. ResETA lifts every level with one correction layer.
First place, SIGSPATIAL 2026 SRC SRC abstract

CoarseETA

ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026

Combines two pieces that are seemingly useless alone: an open-source engine's ranking of trips and the city's coarse zone-to-zone travel statistics. Using public data only, it closes 86 to 87% of the gap to a method that needs exact trip endpoints. Rankings are precomputed and indexed per zone pair, so it adds about 1 ms per query.

  • Query processing
  • Indexing
Preprint GitHub

ETAlon

Live, in progress

A system for benchmarking the ETA query. Every test trip is coarsened down the data ladder, so methods built for different data answer the same queries with the same scorer. So far: 9 methods, 168 runs, and 1.5M test trips in two cities, built with the 8 undergraduate researchers I lead.

  • Benchmarking
  • Evaluation
Try it live

ResETA

In progress

One correction layer that lifts any ETA method at any data level, treating the method as a black box. Coarser isn't just less information. It's more context: where fine data is thin, ResETA leans on the dense statistics of the coarse level.

  • Learned calibration
  • Multi-granular data

TrajSplit

IEEE MDM 2025

Cleans raw GPS streams into single-destination trips with a principled trip definition, plus two heuristics that keep it scalable at above 95% of exhaustive accuracy. Trip-duration error fell from about 150% to under 30%.

  • Data cleaning
  • Scalability
Paper GitHub

Mapedia

SIGSPATIAL 2023 vision

A scalable system for inferring and managing the road metadata OpenStreetMap lacks, with four inference modules over a metadata store.

  • Data inference
  • Metadata management
Vision paper Website

Road Network Genome

SIGSPATIAL 2026 vision

With Mohamed Hemdan, a vision for embedding a whole road network as one vector so regions can share models and data. It calls for multi-scale storage and similarity search over vectors with millions of dimensions.

  • Representation learning
  • Vector search
Preprint

A Grand Tour of ETA Queries

VLDB 2026 tutorial

A survey of 67 papers that rates five families of ETA methods on six criteria, from accuracy and latency to how much historical data they need, and traces the systems behind Google Maps, DiDi, Uber, and Lyft.

  • Survey
  • Cost tradeoffs
