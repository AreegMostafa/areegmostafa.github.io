Areeg Mostafa

I am a PhD candidate in computer science at the University of Minnesota, advised by Mohamed Mokbel.

I work on spatiotemporal data management and open-source mapping systems. The services people rely on every day, travel time estimates, routing, delivery windows, all depend on trip data held by a handful of companies. My research builds the methods and systems that make those services possible from data anyone can get.

Some of my earlier work appears under the name Arig Mostafa.
